Your Sheets. Your Notes. Your Game.

Record Sheet gives you the flexibility of pencil and paper while preserving the convenience of digital files.

Write Anywhere

Add notes, track hit points, record damage, mark inventory, update statistics, or write anywhere on the sheet.

Erase Mistakes

Remove your markings whenever information changes, just like using an eraser on printed paper.

Protect the Original

Your markings are placed over the document, allowing the original PDF or image to remain unchanged.

Use Your Existing Sheets

There is no need to recreate your character or game records inside a system-specific application. Open the sheets you already use and start writing.

  • RPG character sheets
  • Wargame record sheets
  • Vehicle and mech records
  • Maps and handouts
  • Campaign logs and journals
  • Board game score sheets
  • Reference cards
  • Printable game forms

Designed for Tabletop Players

Record Sheet is not tied to one game or rules system. Use it with the documents that work best for your campaign.

Roleplaying Games

Track health, spells, conditions, ammunition, inventory, experience, equipment, and session notes.

Wargames

Mark damage, heat, armor, ammunition, equipment status, unit information, and temporary effects.

System Agnostic

Use Record Sheet with any supported PDF or image-based sheet, regardless of the game system.

Multiple Sheets in One Place

A game session often requires more than one document. Record Sheet allows you to load multiple PDFs and images for characters, vehicles, maps, companions, enemies, reference pages, and campaign notes.

  • Keep related sheets together
  • Switch between game documents
  • Use familiar printable sheets
  • Reduce physical paperwork

Record Sheet is designed to provide a simple digital equivalent of keeping several printed sheets on the table.

How It Works

Open a document, make your markings, and continue playing.

  1. Open Record Sheet.
  2. Import one or more supported PDF or image files.
  3. Use the pencil tool to write, draw, or mark information directly over the sheet.
  4. Use the eraser tool to remove markings that are no longer needed.
  5. Save your work while keeping the original document unchanged.

Think of it as a transparent layer: your markings stay over the document while the original PDF or image remains underneath.
